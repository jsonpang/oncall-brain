[09:45] Wyatt Lawson: Signal 9 is kernel OOMKilled by the node cgroup! Our regional runner container limits are set to 4GB by default in the pod spec.
[09:47] Wyatt Lawson: If you increase JVM heap `-Xmx4g`, the JVM will allocate 4GB plus metaspace and native memory, immediately exceeding the 4GB pod limit and getting killed instantly!
[09:50] Wyatt Lawson: The correct resolution is modifying the pipeline step manifest to raise `resources.limits.memory: 8Gi`, and keep JVM heap at `-Xmx3g`.
[09:52] Dominic Shaw: Ah! Wow, thank you Wyatt. I would have made it worse. Correcting the customer right now with the cgroup configuration.
[09:55] Wyatt Lawson: Always check cgroup limits before adjusting runtime heaps. It's in Doc 06 FAQ section 4.
