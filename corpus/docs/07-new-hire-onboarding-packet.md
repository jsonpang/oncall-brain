# AetherPulse New Hire Operations Onboarding Packet (Standard Template)

## 1. Mandatory Information Delivered to New Hire on Day 1 (Team Lead Checklist)
Every incoming support specialist or operations engineer must be provisioned with the following upon morning arrival:
1. **Designated Onboarding Mentor**: Assigned senior engineer (e.g., Arthur Vance for field engineers, Colin Marsh for pipeline engineers).
2. **Account Credentials**: Corporate single sign-on (SSO), Sentinel Portal agent account, and PulseChat collaboration handles.
3. **Primary Collaboration Channels**: Invitation to `Channel-General-OpsL1L2`, `Channel-OpsSupervision`, and relevant functional sub-channels.
4. **Hardware & Security Setup**: Dedicated operations workstation, PulseVPN credentials, and corporate MFA key.

---

## 2. Independent Action Items for New Hire on Day 1
The new engineer must complete the following actions before 17:00 on Day 1:
- Log in to Sentinel Portal and verify queue visibility for assigned operational tier.
- Join mandatory group channels and update nickname to standard convention: `[Vendor]-[Role]-[Name]`.
- Verify terminal access to internal code repositories and testing sandbox environments.
- Read through Doc 01 (*Service Closed-Loop Mechanism*) and Doc 02 (*Ops Workflow Training Guide*).

---

## 3. Curated Self-Study Curriculum (By Functional Area)
- **Day 1 - 2**: Organizational structure, SLA boundaries, Sentinel ticket lifecycle, and escalation protocols.
- **Day 3 - 4**: Functional module architecture (Pipeline Engine, CI/CD, Container PaaS, Artifact Registry).
- **Day 5**: Common troubleshooting runbooks, simulation exercises, and shadowing active duty specialists.

---

## 4. Daily Learning Summary Protocol
- **Submission Requirement**: Every day before 18:00, the new hire must submit a concise markdown learning brief to their mentor and team lead.
- **Contents**: What was studied, tools accessed, practical questions encountered, and plan for the subsequent day.
- **Validation**: Historical records confirm successful onboarding verification through this daily feedback mechanism.

---

## 5. Mentor and Manager Day 1 Sign-Off
- Mentor Review: Confirmed accounts operational and self-study curriculum initiated.
- Lead Approval: Registered in operational context directory and assigned to shadowing roaster.
