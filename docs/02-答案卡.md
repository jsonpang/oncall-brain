### Q6: 二线升级流程（官方提单 vs 私下沟通）
1. **问题**：  
   `"If I cannot resolve a customer's issue at Tier 1, how do I escalate it to Tier 2? Is it acceptable to message Tier 2 engineers directly?"`
2. **期望状态**：  
   `applicable`
3. **标准答案要点**：  
   - **Next Action (Official Path)**: On Sentinel Desk, click **Transfer** to reassign the ticket to the designated Tier 2 module queue, providing the 4 mandatory transfer fields: 1) Detailed reproduction steps, 2) Captured error messages/screenshots, 3) Affected Regional Operating Territory, and 4) Workarounds attempted with reason for escalation.
   - **Operational Reality (Dual Path)**: Frontline specialists routinely ping known Tier 2 leads (e.g., Colin Marsh for Pipeline) in `Channel-CICD-L1-L2-Collaboration` for rapid diagnostic triage during production blocks.
